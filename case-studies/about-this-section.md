This section is the deliberate side of the site.

**Field Notes** is the working notebook — short entries, written in roughly the
time it takes to make a coffee, usually about something I noticed at the BAS
panel or while a simulation was warming up.

**Case Studies** is the opposite. Each entry here is a thing I want to
understand properly: a piece of theory, a historical episode, a mathematical
object, a paper I read three times and still want to write back to in my own
words. They take weeks. Some of them have nothing to do with what I do for a
living.

A few rules I'm holding myself to:

- **Derive, don't quote.** If a result is in the entry, I should be able to walk
  the reader from a clearly-stated starting point to it. Hand-waving counts as
  unfinished.
- **Show the dead ends.** The interesting part of any serious investigation is
  the path the working notebook took before it landed on the clean explanation.
  When I can reconstruct it, I will.
- **No padding.** Length should be a function of the subject, not a function of
  signaling effort. A 1,200-word entry that says what needs to be said is
  finished.

If you got here from somewhere expecting a portfolio bullet point, the
[project writeups](/#work) are the right place. This is for everything else.
